# Share your app

By default, your app is private and only accessible to you. You can share your app with others by

- making it public and sharing the link
- downloading it and share it the old fashion way
- create a content link and share that
  - this encodes the whole content of your app into the URL
  - useful for sharing small apps quickly

:::tip
To share the app with a hidden canvas/graph

1. Maximise the user interface

![Maximise user interface](/img/screenshots/maximize-user-interface.png)

2. Save the app
3. Next time you or someone else opens the app
   - the canvas and the toolbar are hidden
   - clicking on the _tailrmade_ logo in the top left corner shows the toolbar again

![Maximise user interface](/img/screenshots/home-button.gif)

:::

## Share via public link

1. Sign in to _tailrmade_
   - Learn more about how to [Sign in to tailrmade](/docs/signin)
2. Open the app you want to share
3. Open the "Edit app details" dialog
   - by clicking the "Edit app" button in the apps list
   - OR by pressing `Cmd/Ctrl+E`

![Edit app button](/img/screenshots/edit-app-button.png)

4. Set app access level to "Public"

![App details dialog](/img/screenshots/edit-app-details-public.png)

5. Click "Save"
6. Click on "Copy URL" in
   - the share menu in the top left corner
   - OR on the app name in the apps list

![Copy URL button](/img/screenshots/copy-url-share-menu.png)
![Copy URL button](/img/screenshots/copy-url-button.png)

## Embed in website

1. Follow the steps above to make your app public
2. Use an iframe HTML element to embed your app in any website

```html
<iframe
  src="YOUR_APPS_PUBLIC_URL"
  width="800"
  height="600"
  style="border: none; border-radius: 8px;"
  title="Tailrmade App Embed"
  allow="camera"
  loading="lazy"
>
</iframe>
```

Replace `YOUR_APPS_PUBLIC_URL` with your app's actual public URL.

### Customization options

You can customize how the embedded app appears:

- `width` and `height`: Set dimensions (pixels or percentage)
- `style`: Add custom CSS (border, border-radius, box-shadow, etc.)

## Download the app

1. Open the app you want to download
2. In the share menu on the top left, click on
   - "Download (Plain text)" - JSON file
   - OR "Download (Compressed)" - compressed file
3. The file is downloaded in your browser's default download location

## Share via content link

1. In the share menu on the top left, click on "Share app embedded in link"
   - OR press `Cmd/Ctrl+K`
2. The app is encoded into the URL and copied to your clipboard

:::info

- Max URL size is around 5 MB
- Content links can get very long depending on the size of your app
- Some platforms may not support pasting very long URLs
  :::
