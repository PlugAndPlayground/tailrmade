# Sign in to _tailrmade_

You do not need an account to use _tailrmade_. However, signing in allows you to

- access your apps from any device
- share your apps with others via a public link
- use our `Claude API` and `Gemini API` nodes
- use our AI assistant
- [securely store API keys](/docs/access-apis#cloud-companion-recommended) for use in HTTP nodes

## Create an account

1. Click on the "My account" button in the top left corner

![My account button](/img/screenshots/my-account-button.png)

2. Click on "Need an account? Sign up"
3. Fill in your email and password
4. Click "Sign up"

![Sign up dialog](/img/screenshots/sign-up-dialog.png)

## Sign in to _tailrmade_

1. Click on the "My account" button in the top left corner
2. Fill in your email and password
3. Click "Sign in"

![Sign in dialog](/img/screenshots/sign-in-dialog.png)

## Reset your password

1. Click on the "My account" button in the top left corner
2. Click on "Forgot password?"
3. Enter your email and click "Send reset email"
4. Check your email and click on the link
5. Enter a new password and confirm it

![Reset password dialog](/img/screenshots/reset-password-dialog.png)

## Current account limits

| Storage           | 1 MB      | 1 GB storage |
| AI tokens per day | Included  | Higher limit |

AI limits are shown as Tailrmade tokens, which normalize each AI model's
provider cost into one shared usage budget.
